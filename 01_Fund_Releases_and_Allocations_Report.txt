1. OVERVIEW & FUND FLOW FRAMEWORK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Under the Members of Parliament Local Area Development Scheme (MPLADS), each MP 
is entitled to recommend works up to Rs. 5 Crore per annum. Following the 
eSAKSHI revised fund-flow procedure (effective April 1, 2023):
- Entitlements are released in online authorisations via Central Nodal Agency (CNA).
- Funds are non-lapsable; unspent balances roll over to subsequent fiscal years.
- Funds are maintained through digital authorizations directly integrated with 
  PFMS (Public Financial Management System).

4. COMPLIANCE & ALLOCATION RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Scheduled Caste (SC) Earmarking : Minimum 15.0% of total annual entitlement
- Scheduled Tribe (ST) Earmarking : Minimum 7.5% of total annual entitlement
- Trust/Society Cap             : Maximum Rs 75 Lakh per asset for eligible trusts
- Non-Lapsable Account Rule    : All authorizations carry over across fiscal years
