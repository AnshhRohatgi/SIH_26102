1. UTILIZATION CERTIFICATE (UC) PROTOCOL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A Utilization Certificate (UC) is a formal statutory accounting statement issued by 
the Implementing District Authority / Collector to certify that funds authorized by 
the Central Nodal Agency (MoSPI) have been deployed strictly for approved works.

- Prerequisites for Next Tranche Release:
  1. Submission of UC for at least 80% of previously authorized funds.
  2. Proof of physical completion of past projects.
  3. Interest accrual report and unspent balance declaration.
