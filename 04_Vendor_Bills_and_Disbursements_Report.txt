1. DIGITAL DISBURSEMENT WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Under the eSAKSHI digital system:
1. Contractors / Implementing Agencies upload milestone vendor bills on eSAKSHI.
2. District Authority inspects work stage and verifies physical progress.
3. Geo-tagged & timestamped photos are uploaded via the eSAKSHI Mobile App.
4. Electronic payments are processed directly to contractor bank accounts via PFMS.
